# Snap Recomp 1.4.0
## Known issues

- Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC" because the exe is not code-signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

## Saves and settings carry over

Updating keeps your saves and settings. Before anything changes, the updater copies them to a dated folder named update-backups next to the game's data and keeps the newest 3 copies. Going back to the previous version also makes a copy first. A portable install (portable.txt next to the exe) keeps everything in its own portable_data folder and carries it across the update.

## Driver notes

- NVIDIA: no known driver problems. Tested on driver version 621.14 with an RTX 4090.

## Changes

- Setup's detection screen now lists the game files it found even when the scan stops early. Before, it could say "Found 0 game file(s)" while the log said 1 ([#1](https://github.com/finalpantasy/new-pokemon-snap-recomp/issues/1)).

Also in this release:

Smoother play. The shader cache stays in memory, so a ride starts about 5 seconds sooner. Several causes of random mid-ride stalls (180 to 500 ms) are fixed. A save no longer holds up the game while it writes, and a save deadlock is fixed. Several of the game's busiest functions now run as native code. Gamepads are found on a background thread, so plugging one in no longer freezes a frame.

Graphics options. HD effects renders the sea, particles and bloom at your render resolution. HD shadows has three settings, Off, Upscaled and Re-baked, and every course now has a calibrated Re-baked shadow set. A new sparkle filter calms the shimmering highlights you see when the camera moves. Most graphics rows now apply live, with no restart: internal resolution, anti-aliasing, shadow resolution, ultrawide and the rest. The Frame generation row offers NVIDIA DLSS. The HD texture pack builder can build every category from your own dump and fills in the color under the clear parts of cut-out textures.

Options and input. The in-game menu has 19 tabs, with labels that match the launcher. New aim feel options cover the stick curve, dead zone, gyro smoothing and raw mouse input. Gamepads that fail to open are retried every 3 seconds. Window size, sound and controller settings can be set from the command line, and so can `--restore-backup`, `--upscalers` and `--hdtex`. A Steam Deck preset and a Device row in the launcher set it up in one step.

Audio. Gaps in the sound fade out instead of clicking, a limiter keeps peaks from clipping, volume changes slide at a fixed rate, and there are new options for latency, resampler, range and output device.

Window and menus. The picture follows the window when you resize it. The menu scale is clamped so the menu always fits the window, and the cursor's orange arc no longer blinks above 60 fps. Album export makes a dated folder and reports a write that failed.

Safety. Mod packs with unsafe ids are refused, and zips that would expand far past their declared size are stopped. The updater turns a disk error during a download into a clear message, and a rollback also puts back the exe you start the game with.

## Install or update

New install: put SnapRecomp.exe in a folder of its own and run it. Update: open the launcher and choose Updates. Version 1.2.0 and earlier cannot update to a one-exe release; download SnapRecomp.exe by hand, and your saves are copied over on the first start. The Testing channel in the launcher's Updates page also offers early builds; Stable is the default.
