# fixture: post_tutorial
# Free camera on Florio (docs/freecam.md): ride, switch the free camera on at the rail pose, fly to off-rail poses with fcpose (offsets from the rail
# camera when the camera went on: right, up, forward, yaw, pitch), save clean PNGs (fcshot) and frame dumps (shot=1), then switch it off and ride on.
# Run: powershell -File host\run_boot.ps1 -BuildDir build\w\freecam -Route freecam_florio -Timeout 420 -ShotAt 9999 -ShotMax 40
# Pass: boot_stderr.log shows "[freecam] on", clean shots saved, "[freecam] off", the rail progress after "off" larger than at "on", "end of route reached".
repeat @f300 @f30000 120 8 A
wait ui=map_select timeout=240
repeat @f60 @f30000 120 8 A
wait ride timeout=240
# the rail camera before the switch (reference frame)
@f240 2 shot=1
# on at the rail pose, pose held, then off-rail poses
@f300 1150 fc=on
@f340 2 shot=1
@f400 200 fcpose=0,6,-8,0,-15
@f470 2 fcshot
@f480 2 shot=1
@f620 200 fcpose=-12,2,6,-50,0
@f690 2 fcshot
@f700 2 shot=1
@f840 200 fcpose=0,60,-40,0,-45
@f910 2 fcshot
@f920 2 shot=1
@f1060 200 fcpose=0,-40,0,0,10
@f1130 2 fcshot
@f1140 2 shot=1
@f1280 120 fcpose=0,0,400,0,0
@f1340 2 fcshot
@f1350 2 shot=1
@f1470 30 fc=off
# back on the rail: the ride goes on
@f1600 2 shot=1
@f1900 2 shot=1
end @f2000
